Introduction

When Fear Becomes Familiar

Fear rarely enters loudly. More often, fear arrives quietly, blending into routine thoughts and familiar reactions. Over time, fear can begin to feel normal. Even responsible. Even spiritual.

Many believers reach a place where they still love Jesus, still believe the Word, and still affirm truth, yet live internally governed by anxiety.

This book invites readers to unlearn fear’s voice and relearn the voice of Jesus.
