Introduction

When Fear Becomes Familiar

Fear rarely enters loudly. More often, fear arrives quietly, blending into routine thoughts and familiar reactions. Over time, fear can begin feeling normal. Even responsible. Even spiritual.

Many believers still love Jesus deeply while quietly living internally governed by anxiety, caution, and fear driven thinking.

This book invites readers to unlearn fear’s voice and relearn the voice of Jesus.
