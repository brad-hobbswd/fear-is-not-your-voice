Closing Prayer

Fear Is Not My Voice

A final prayer of surrender, healing, trust, peace, and confidence in the authority of Jesus.

Jesus, thank You for never leaving me governed by fear. Thank You for giving power to stand, love to remain rooted, and a sound mind to discern truth clearly.

I release every voice that attempted leading me away from trust in You. I release anxiety, striving, suspicion, and fear driven thinking. Every fearful agreement loses authority through Your mighty name, Jesus.

Teach my heart to live from peace instead of panic. Teach my mind to remain anchored in truth instead of controlled by uncertainty. Teach my spirit to recognize Your voice above every competing voice.

Thank You for Your finished work upon the cross. Thank You for resurrection power still carrying authority today. Thank You for remaining faithful through every storm, every correction, every waiting season, and every fearful moment.

I choose trust. I choose surrender. I choose agreement with Your Word.

Fear is not my voice.
Fear is not my guide.
Fear is not my authority.

Jesus, You are.

In Your mighty name, Jesus, amen.
